Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Retail Order Processing & Dispatch

For retail online orders, if an item is in stock on our store, that batch has already been handcrafted, quality-checked, and is ready to be dispatched within 24 hours. We process dispatches Monday through Saturday to ensure your sweets reach you as fresh as possible.

2. Corporate & Bulk Orders

Corporate, customized, and bulk orders follow a dedicated production and fulfillment policy:

  • Fresh Batch Production: Bulk confectionery is made to order upon confirmation to maximize freshness and shelf life.
  • Custom Branding & Packaging: Custom sleeves, personalized greeting cards, and bespoke ribbons require 2–4 business days for printing and assembly.
  • Flexible Logistics: We support both centralized bulk freight delivery to your corporate headquarters and multi-address direct dispatch to individual employee or client residences across India.

3. Delivery Timelines (1 to 5 Days)

Once dispatched, orders typically reach your doorstep within 1 to 5 business days based on your delivery destination:

Destination Estimated Delivery Time
Bengaluru / Same City 1 – 2 Business Days
Major Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata) 2 – 3 Business Days
Tier 2 / Rest of India 3 – 5 Business Days

4. Trusted Delivery Partners

To guarantee safe, timely, and temperature-conscious transit across India, we partner with premier express logistics networks including Delhivery and Shiprocket. As soon as your order is picked up, you will receive real-time tracking details via email and SMS.

5. Shipping Charges & Free Delivery

We believe in simple, transparent shipping rates:

  • Free Shipping: All retail orders with a net total of ₹999 or above qualify for 100% FREE delivery across India.
  • Standard Shipping: A flat shipping charge of ₹50 applies to orders below ₹999.

6. Temperature-Safe & Protective Packaging

Because our bites contain rich couverture chocolate, pure dry fruits, and zero artificial preservatives, we package all shipments in sturdy, shock-absorbing rigid boxes with food-grade protective cushioning to protect against transit temperature and handling.

7. Order Tracking & Support

You can track the live status of your shipment using the tracking link sent upon dispatch or directly in your SweetScribbles account profile.

For any queries regarding delayed dispatches, address corrections, or corporate shipping logistics, please reach out to us at: Pooja.sathish@pikachooz.com.
