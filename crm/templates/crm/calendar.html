Live 2-Way Calendar Subscription

Subscribe once, and all current and future Client Meetings, Delivery Deadlines, and Office Events in Sweet Scribbles CRM will automatically sync to your personal Google Calendar, Apple Calendar (Mac/iPhone), or Outlook.

1. Open Google Calendar in your browser.
2. On the left sidebar, click the + next to Other calendars.
3. Select From URL.
4. Paste the copied URL and click Add calendar.

1. Open the Calendar app on Mac.
2. In the top menu bar, click File → New Calendar Subscription...
3. Paste the URL and click Subscribe.
4. Set auto-refresh to Every hour.
